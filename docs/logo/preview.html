Verge — header lockup concepts

Each shown at real header scale (mark 48px, wordmark 24px Inter Semibold) on the app's canvas, with the mark enlarged beside it. "Verge" in Australian English is the strip of planting between the footpath and the road — the concepts lean on that rather than on a generic leaf.

The verge itself
Planting rising from the kerb line. The most literal reading of the name, and the closest to what you have now — but grounded, so it reads as a place rather than a plant.
